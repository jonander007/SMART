What is this survey?

This questionnaire applies the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) developed by Thomas Saaty. You will be asked to compare criteria, subcriteria and rating scale values in pairs using a 1–9 importance scale. Your responses will be used to derive priority weights for a multi-criteria decision analysis.

Alternatives under evaluation

SEALER-55MMRKP-FHR

How to answer

  1. Review the Alternatives and Hierarchy tabs to familiarise yourself with the study.
  2. Click Start Survey to begin.
  3. For each pair, move the slider towards the element you consider more important.
  4. When finished, click Download Results to save your responses.
